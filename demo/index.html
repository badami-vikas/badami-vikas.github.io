One core. A business's own way of working on top.

These businesses run processes too specific for anyone to build software for. Zazoo learns how each one works and runs it with agents, on the business's own machine, maintained by us. Each demo is a made-up business with made-up data: click anything. Every one has Agents, Tasks, a Calendar, a Knowledge base and the Second Brain.

Clients on a map of the state; insurer letters read and filed, appeal deadlines computed, physician questionnaires drafted for the attorney, scanned checks matched without touching trust until the bank confirms. Intake, litigation, team workload and a fee forecast too.

00 Site intelligence for wash operators

Find, finance and build your next wash. Then keep every store on plan.

WashBridge is a team of agents working for your development team. It reads every parcel in your market, applies your rules, finds the owners of sites that aren't for sale, and forecasts each site on your own stores' data. Then it stays with the site through financing, the build and every year after opening.

    Then it runs the build, and watches every store you already run.

    The build tracker reads permit portals and contractor updates and flags anything that moves opening day. Once open, the store joins the portfolio monitor: volume against forecast, members, churn and new rivals. When a store drifts, you get the reason and a next move.

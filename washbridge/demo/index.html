One site core. Your chain's own way of choosing sites on top.

Wash chains choose sites with rules too specific for generic real estate software. WashBridge learns your gates and weights and runs them with agents, on your own machine, maintained by us. This demo is a made-up chain with made-up sites.

A car wash chain choosing sites

Every parcel in the metro run through the chain's own gates and weights, on a map with its stores and competitors; traffic, households, zoning and a pro forma for each site. A CRM for owners and brokers, and a forecast with new sites at their ramp.
